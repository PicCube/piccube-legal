Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how PicCube (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information when you use the PicCube iOS application (Bundle ID: com.piccube.bzketu).

Contact: Piccubemail@gmail.com

1. Permissions

PicCube may ask for:

  • Camera — when you choose Take Photo for a cube face or journal entry.
  • Microphone — when you record a voice keepsake or challenge reward.
  • Photo Library — when you choose photos from your library for faces or content, and Photo Library Add when you save a composite to Photos.
  • Notifications — requested on first visit to Desk (Home). PicCube sends notifications related to the features you use in the app, according to how the app works. You can turn them off at any time in iOS Settings.

You can change permission status in iOS Settings. Denying a permission disables only the features that need it.

2. Purchases and Cube Credits

Optional Cube Credits packs are sold through Apple In-App Purchase (StoreKit). Payment is processed by Apple. We do not receive or store your full payment card numbers. Credit balances and related ledgers are maintained for your use of the app. Credits and unlocks are removed if you use Delete All Data or delete the app; Apple’s purchase history remains with your Apple ID under Apple’s policies.

3. Overview

PicCube lets you build photo cubes, author and play keepsake challenges, keep studio notes and inspiration, and purchase Cube Credits for optional features such as exporting challenge packs and unlocking shapes. This policy explains what information is involved, how it is used, and the choices you have.

4. Facial data

4.1 What is collected

PicCube does not create biometric faceprints, face templates, or face-geometry models. You may choose to add photos for cube faces, journals, inspiration, or challenge rewards. Those photos may depict a face if a face appears in the image you select or capture.

4.2 Planned use

Photos that may include a face are used only to display and assemble cube faces, studio entries, challenge rewards, archive icons, and related in-app previews you request.

4.3 Sharing, disclosure, and storage

Face-bearing photos you add stay under your control in the content you create. PicCube does not sell this content. If you export a challenge pack or save an image to Photos, the files you choose to share or save leave the app according to the destination you pick (for example Files, AirDrop, Messages, or the Photos library). Imported packs you open may include photos provided by the pack’s maker.

4.4 Retention

Photos and related cube media remain until you delete the project, clear them in-feature, use Delete All Data, or remove the app (which removes app sandbox data). Exported copies you saved elsewhere follow that destination’s retention rules.

4.5 Explanation of this chapter

This Section 4 is the explanation of PicCube’s collection, use, disclosure, sharing, and retention practices for facial data as that term applies to user photos that may depict a face. PicCube does not perform face recognition or build biometric identifiers.

5. Challenge packs and sharing

When you export a .piccube pack, the pack can include face images, scramble moves, timer settings, and keepsake text, audio, or photo rewards you attached. Recipients who import the pack can play the challenge and, after solving, view the reward content included in the pack.

6. Information we process

Depending on how you use PicCube, the app may process:

  • Account and session preferences — such as whether you have tapped Get Started, nickname display settings, and related session flags stored with the app.
  • Cube and challenge content — face photos you select or capture, scramble and play progress, archive names, challenge timers, keepsake notes, voice recordings, optional reward photos, and exported or imported .piccube challenge packs.
  • Studio content — mood journal entries, inspiration pins, gratitude lines, and goal breakdown items you create.
  • Virtual currency records — Cube Credit balances, ledger entries, shape unlocks, and purchase-related status shown in the app after Apple processes a StoreKit purchase.
  • Device permissions data — camera frames, microphone audio, photo-library images, and notification authorization status, only when you grant the related iOS permission and use the matching feature.
  • Diagnostics you choose to send — if you email us, your message and contact details are processed so we can respond.

We do not require a separate social-network account to use core cube-making features.

7. How we use information

We use the information above to:

  • Operate cube creation, play, archive, Create Level, import/export, Studio tools, and Profile settings.
  • Apply Cube Credits for features you choose (for example Share Challenge Pack or Save Pack to Files).
  • Request and respect notification permission so PicCube can send notifications related to the features you use in the app, according to how the app works. You can turn them off at any time in iOS Settings.
  • Improve reliability and respond to support requests you send to Piccubemail@gmail.com.
  • Comply with applicable law and App Store rules.

8. Delete All Data and your choices

In Profile you can:

  • End Session — return to the Get Started entry experience while keeping credits and unlocks on the device.
  • Delete All Data — permanently erase Cube Credits, unlocks, projects, archives, studio content, media files, and related preferences stored by the app on this device.

You may also delete individual archives or levels from Desk, remove media in editors, or uninstall the app.

9. Third-party services

  • Apple — App Store distribution, StoreKit purchases, and system sharing sheets you invoke.

PicCube does not send your prompts or reference images to an external automated generation or analysis service as part of core features.

10. Children’s privacy

PicCube is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact Piccubemail@gmail.com and we will take appropriate steps.

11. Data sale

We do not sell your personal information.

12. International users

PicCube is offered through the App Store. Information processed for the features above is handled as described in this policy and in accordance with Apple’s platform rules.

13. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
